Explore a puzzle

  1. Choose the bounding box, enable partitions, and enter their rows or edit their diagrams.
  2. Select allowed tiles, then choose Compute puzzles.
  3. Choose a numbered result or Overview. Right-drag the canvas to rotate; click a puzzle in the overview to inspect it.

Partitions are read from bottom/left to top/right. Use Arrows in Display to see their orientation.

Keyboard shortcuts

0–9 views · Space animate · Enter rotate 90° · ←/→ results · ↑/↓ calculations.

On touch screens, enable Swap pointer actions below the canvas to rotate by dragging.
